## What SimpleCommenter charges for

SimpleCommenter gets onto a site three ways: a script snippet, a WordPress plugin or a Chrome extension. Clients then comment on the live page, images or PDFs, and each comment carries a status from To Do to Done. Pro at $34.99 covers 10 projects and three members, with integrations, automatic screenshots and an MCP service. Agency at $79 lifts every cap and adds white label and a custom domain. There is no free plan; the 14-day trial is the only free window.

The [head-to-head with SimpleCommenter](/vs/simplecommenter) goes row by row. This page is for choosing between the alternatives.

## Which one fits which job

MarkLayer is the free replacement for what most SimpleCommenter projects come down to: comments pinned on a live site, shared by link. It adds live cursors, an in-room call and an agent on every link. It gives up the on-site widget and white label.

Pastel is the paid tool shaped most like SimpleCommenter for agencies, with a free plan to try. ruttl is the flat-rate option for a small team that wants unlimited projects. Feedbucket fits when the client count is high and the internal seat count is low. If the thing you would pay SimpleCommenter for is white label on your own domain, Agency at $79 is the cheapest of the four to offer it, and Pastel and Feedbucket do not advertise it at all.

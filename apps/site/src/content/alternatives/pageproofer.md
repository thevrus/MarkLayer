## What PageProofer charges for

PageProofer is a feedback layer on your own site: notes pinned to the page, each with a screenshot, the element's selector, the browser and any console errors, plus screen recordings and a status for every note. All of that is on every plan. What the tiers change is the user count: five on Freelancer at $39, 25 on Team at $89, unlimited on Agency at $199, which also adds custom branding. There is no free plan and never has been. The 15-day trial is the only free window.

The [head-to-head with PageProofer](/vs/pageproofer) goes row by row. This page is for choosing between the alternatives.

## Which one fits which job

MarkLayer is the free replacement for the part of PageProofer most people pay for: pin feedback on the live page and send a link. It adds live cursors, an in-room call and an agent on every link. It gives up screen recordings and console capture.

BugHerd is the paid tool shaped most like PageProofer, with a kanban board behind the feedback tab and tracker sync on its top plan. Marker.io fits when reports need console logs and network requests. Pastel suits agencies that want a client-facing workspace with a free plan to start on. If the thing you would pay PageProofer for is unlimited users with custom branding, Agency at $199 is competitive with BugHerd's Premium at $150 for 25.

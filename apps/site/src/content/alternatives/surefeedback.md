## What SureFeedback sells and what it costs

SureFeedback is two products under one name. Cloud is a managed service: a script on each reviewed site loads the widget, and the agency pays by site count, $199 then $299 a year for five sites. The self-hosted plugin is a WordPress install you run yourself, priced by dashboard site, $99 then $119 for one. Both include private team-only comments, page approvals, a kanban board and mockup review on every plan. Neither has a free tier, and the renewal is higher than the first-year price on every plan.

The [head-to-head with SureFeedback](/vs/surefeedback) goes row by row. This page is for choosing between the alternatives.

## Which one fits which job

MarkLayer is the free replacement for the part most people use: annotate a live page, share a link, collect comments. It adds live cursors and an in-room call, which SureFeedback does not advertise, and an agent on every link. It gives up private comments, per-page approvals, mockups and the WordPress-hosted data.

Atarim is the nearest paid match for a WordPress agency: branded client workspace, plugin and extension, comments that become tasks with screenshots. Most of its $67 now pays for AI agents that maintain client sites, so it is a different purchase than SureFeedback's $199. BugHerd is a bug tracker with a client-facing board, and its private commenting needs the $150 tier. Pastel is the agency review workspace for teams not on WordPress.

If the thing you would pay SureFeedback for is feedback stored inside your own WordPress database, nothing here replaces it, and the plugin at $99 is a fair price.

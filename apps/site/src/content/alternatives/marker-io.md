---

Marker.io is built for development and agency teams that want visual bug reports to land directly in the tracker they already run: Jira, GitHub, and integrations with project management tools like Trello, Linear, Asana, and ClickUp, plus a dozen others. Install the feedback widget or browser extension, someone circles a broken button, and the ticket appears in the backlog with a screenshot and the page URL attached. That pipeline is the entire pitch, and it works well for teams that already live inside a tracker all day.

It also costs money from day one, and the entry price doesn't buy what most people assume it does. The Starter plan runs $59 a month billed monthly, or $39 a month if you commit to a year, for three team members and up to ten projects. Jira sync specifically isn't part of that tier: it only unlocks on the Team plan, which jumps to $199 a month billed monthly ($149 annually) for 15 users. Marker.io offers a 15-day free trial and nothing free after it ends.

That combination, no free tier plus a second price jump just to reach Jira, is what sends people searching for a Marker.io alternative in the first place. Some just want to mark up a design for a client and don't want a subscription for a handful of reports a month. Others already pay for a tracker and don't want to pay again for the pipe that feeds it. If your team's real bottleneck is turning feedback into tickets automatically, the paid options below stay the honest answer. If it's getting a mark and a comment onto a page and sharing the link, the free routes cover it — the marker.io alternatives below are ranked by how much of that pipeline they replace.

Userback bundles three things product teams often buy separately: an embeddable feedback widget with annotated screenshots, a bug-tracking dashboard, and session replay with NPS surveys layered on top. It's built for product and QA teams who want one tool that captures a user's screenshot, console errors, and browser context, then routes the report into Jira or ClickUp without anyone retyping what happened. If your team just needs to mark up a page and argue about a button placement, Userback's survey and roadmap tooling is more than the job requires.

Userback now ships a genuine free tier. Free Forever covers 2 seats and 2 projects, and holds feedback for 7 days before it locks - a real cap, not a trial clock. Team, at $29/month billed annually ($39 month-to-month), lifts that to 5 seats, 5 projects, and unlimited retention, plus two-way Jira and ClickUp sync and Zapier. Session replay, the AI feedback classifier, surveys, automations, and developer tools like console and network log capture all sit behind Business, $79/month annually ($99 monthly), which also removes the seat limit entirely. Business Plus, $159/month annually ($199 monthly), adds a mobile SDK, REST API, webhooks, and the Intercom/Zendesk integrations most support teams actually want.

The catch with the free plan is that 7-day window: a report opened on a Monday client call is unreadable by the following Tuesday, which pushes teams onto Team just to keep a normal review cycle alive. And Userback's widget is built to run on a product you own and can drop a script tag into - it doesn't help when you need to mark up a client's live site, a staging environment you don't control, or a competitor's page during research. That's the gap the alternatives below fill, MarkLayer's included: free annotation on any URL, not just the one you've instrumented.

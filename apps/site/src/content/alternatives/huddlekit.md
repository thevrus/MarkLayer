## What Huddlekit's free plan does and doesn't cover

One project, three team members, 100MB of storage, unlimited guests. For a single client site that is enough, and the guest commenting is as frictionless as it says: guests get a share link and do not sign up. The cap bites on project two. Custom breakpoints, tags, folders and MCP are Pro. Integrations and API access are Team. Private comments, inspect mode and the kanban board are on every plan, including Free, which is more generous than most tools in this category.

The [head-to-head with Huddlekit](/vs/huddlekit) goes row by row. This page is for choosing between the alternatives.

## Which one fits which job

MarkLayer is the free replacement for the part of Huddlekit most people actually use: annotate a live page, share a link. It adds live cursors and an in-room call, which Huddlekit does not advertise, and an agent on every link. It gives up private comments, video, custom breakpoints and sync.

Pastel is the paid tool shaped most like Huddlekit, with canvases per client and branded review pages. BugHerd and Marker.io are bug trackers more than review tools: BugHerd for a built-in task board that syncs with Jira, Marker.io for console and network capture attached to every report. If the thing you would pay Huddlekit for is the Linear or Asana sync, Pastel and BugHerd are the comparable spend. If it is the video review or the private comments, none of the four replace it, and Huddlekit's Pro at $16 is a fair price.

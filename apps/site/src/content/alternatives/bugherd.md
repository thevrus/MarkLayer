Looking for a free [BugHerd](https://bugherd.com) alternative? BugHerd is a feedback management solution that turns visual feedback into a Kanban-style bug tracker: someone clicks a pin on a live page, and it automatically captures a screenshot plus the browser, OS, screen resolution, and CSS selector, then drops the whole thing into a task column next to the rest of your backlog. That's the pitch for agencies and QA teams who need a client's comment to become an assignable ticket, with an owner and a status, rather than a comment thread someone has to triage by hand.

There's no free plan. Only a trial, backed by a 60-day money-back guarantee. Standard runs $50/month for 5 members ($42/month billed annually), with extra seats at $8 each. Studio moves to $80/month for 10 members, and Premium is $150/month for 25; a custom Enterprise tier adds SSO and a dedicated success manager. Every tier includes the same Kanban board and the same 20-plus integrations (Jira, Trello, Asana, ClickUp, GitHub, Linear, Slack, MS Teams). What changes as you move up the tiers is mostly seat count.

That pricing works fine when an agency is billing a client for QA. It's a much harder sell for a two-person team that just wants to circle a broken button and drop a link into Linear. If you don't need the task board, the automatic metadata capture, or the native integrations, the annotation half of BugHerd is available for free elsewhere.

## What changed at Markup.io

Markup.io discontinued its free tier in early 2025 and moved Pro from $29 to $79 a month. Teams that built their review process on the free plan did not choose to start paying. The plan stopped existing. That is a different risk from a price increase, and it is why MarkLayer has no paid tier to discontinue a free one into.

Current tiers and the history behind them are in the [Markup.io pricing guide](/guides/markup-io-pricing). For a feature-by-feature read, see the [Markup.io review](/vs/markup-io).

## Which one fits which job

MarkLayer is the direct free replacement for Markup.io's core job: annotate a live page, share a link, no account on either side. The other three annotators are paid routes. PageProofer suits staging-site review, BugHerd turns feedback into tracked tickets, and Pastel runs structured agency client rounds, though Pastel does have a free plan capped at 1 user and 1 canvas. Hypothesis sits outside both groups. It annotates text in documents, not a page's visual layout.

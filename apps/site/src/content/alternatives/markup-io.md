Looking for a free [Markup.io](https://markup.io) alternative? Markup.io discontinued its free tier in early 2025 and moved Pro from $29 to $79 per month, and a lot of teams went shopping after that change landed. Below are the strongest options as of September 2026, ranked by how cleanly they replace the core Markup.io workflow: visual feedback on live websites with link-based sharing.

That pricing move is worth sitting with for a second, because it's the whole reason this page exists. A team that built its review process around Markup.io's free plan didn't choose to start paying $79 a month; the plan they were on simply stopped existing. That's a different kind of risk than a price increase, and it's part of why MarkLayer has no paid tier at all to eventually discontinue a free one into.

The five options below split into two groups. MarkLayer is the direct free replacement for Markup.io's core job: annotate a live page, share a link, no account on either side. PageProofer, BugHerd, and Pastel are the paid routes, each replacing a different piece of what a project-based platform like Markup.io offered beyond plain annotation, staging-site workflows, ticket tracking, or agency-grade client review. Hypothesis sits outside both groups entirely, since it annotates text in documents rather than a page's visual layout.

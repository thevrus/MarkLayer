## What Atarim's free plan does and doesn't cover

One project on one site, 200 credits that do not refill, and workflows that are configured but dormant. For a single client site that is enough to try the commenting. The cap bites on project two, or when the credits run out. Pro brings 5,000 credits a month and up to 1,000 projects, and its price is for the six AI specialists, the workflow library and the white-labelled client dashboard. If the thing you wanted was a comment pinned to an element, $67 a month buys a lot you may not use.

The [head-to-head with Atarim](/vs/atarim) goes row by row. This page is for choosing between the alternatives.

## Which one fits which job

MarkLayer is the free replacement for the feedback part: annotate a live page, share a link, work the threads on a board. It adds live cursors and an in-room call, which Atarim does not advertise, and an agent on every link. It gives up the WordPress plugin, the client branding and the AI agents.

SureFeedback is the paid tool shaped most like the Atarim of a few years ago: WordPress agencies, client approvals, private comments, feedback on your own server. BugHerd and Marker.io are bug trackers more than review tools: BugHerd for a built-in task board that syncs with Jira, Marker.io for console and network capture attached to every report. If the thing you would pay Atarim for is the Asana, ClickUp or Monday sync, BugHerd's Premium tier is the comparable spend. If it is AI agents maintaining client WordPress sites under your brand, none of the four replace it, and Atarim is the only tool here that sells it.

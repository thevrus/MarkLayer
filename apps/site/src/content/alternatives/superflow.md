## What Superflow charges for

Superflow has two products in one price list. The human side pins comments on a live site, with clients reviewing over a link and no seat. The AI side scans pages and posts findings, sold either at $14 a month per site or per scan on credits. Starter is free but covers one small-site scan. Growth at $29 a seat buys two full projects with rescans. Scale at $34 buys four, or portfolio monitoring. Both numbers drop by about $5 on yearly billing.

The [head-to-head with Superflow](/vs/superflow) goes row by row. This page is for choosing between the alternatives.

## Which one fits which job

MarkLayer is the free replacement for the human side: comments on the real page, shared by link, with live cursors and a call in the room. It does not scan pages. What it has instead is an MCP server on every link, so the coding agent you already pay for reads the pins, fixes the code and resolves them.

Pastel suits agencies that want canvases per client, approvals and versions, with a free plan to try. A small team that wants unlimited projects at a flat rate should look at ruttl. Userback is for feedback that should come from your own users through a widget. If the thing you would pay Superflow for is the automated scan, none of the four replace it, and $14 a site is the number to judge.

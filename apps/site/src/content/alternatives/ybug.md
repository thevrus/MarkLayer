## What Ybug's free plan does and doesn't cover

One project, one team member, 50 screenshots a month, unlimited reporters. The widget drops in with a snippet, or reporters use the browser extension, and each report carries browser, OS, screen size, URL and, from Basic up, console logs. For one site and one developer that is enough. The cap bites on the second project or the second teammate, and the capture that makes a bug report worth filing, full console and network requests, starts on Startup at €23.

The [head-to-head with Ybug](/vs/ybug) goes row by row. This page is for choosing between the alternatives.

## Which one fits which job

MarkLayer is the free replacement for the review half of Ybug: a reviewer opens the page, pins feedback, sends a link. It adds live cursors and an in-room call, which Ybug does not advertise, and an agent on every link. It gives up the feedback button on your own site, console capture and recordings.

Marker.io is the paid tool shaped most like Ybug, with the same widget-or-extension choice and console logs on every report. Choose Userback when two free seats matter more than capture depth, and BugHerd when feedback should become tracked tasks on a board. If the thing you would pay Ybug for is console and network capture, Ybug's Startup at €23 for seven members is cheaper than any of the three.

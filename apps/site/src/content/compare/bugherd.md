MarkLayer and [BugHerd](https://bugherd.com) both let you annotate web pages with arrows and comments. The difference: BugHerd is a full visual bug-tracking platform with project management, integrations, and team workflows. MarkLayer is a free, lightweight annotation tool focused on quick visual feedback. No Kanban board, no integrations, no account.

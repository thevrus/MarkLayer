MarkLayer and [Feedbucket](https://feedbucket.app) solve website feedback from opposite ends. Feedbucket is a script you add to sites you own: reviewers get a feedback button that captures annotated screenshots and video, routed into your tracker. MarkLayer is a free browser extension pointed at any page, including ones you could never add a script to, with a share link instead of an install.

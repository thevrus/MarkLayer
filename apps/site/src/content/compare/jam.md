MarkLayer and [Jam.dev](https://jam.dev) are both Chrome extensions for the broad category of "feedback on a web page", but they target different jobs. Jam is a developer-focused bug reporter. One click captures the page state plus console errors, network logs, and reproduction steps for engineers. MarkLayer is a free visual annotation tool. Drawings, arrows, threaded comments, live cursors. Built for design review, client feedback, and lightweight QA.

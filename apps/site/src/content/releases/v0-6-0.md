description: 'MarkLayer 0.6.0 posts new annotations to Slack, Teams, Discord or any webhook, adds a full-screen triage board, an approved status owned by the requester, and presenter mode.'
summary: >-
  Feedback now leaves the page on its own: connect a room to Slack, Teams, Discord or a URL and new comments arrive
  where the team already works. A full-screen board sorts everything by status, and a thread finally has an ending
  the person who asked for the change owns.
changes:
  - kind: new
    title: Send annotations out
    text: >-
      Connect a room to Slack, Microsoft Teams, Discord or any URL of your own, and new comments post there while
      nobody has the tab open. A burst of feedback arrives as one message instead of twenty, and the room link comes
      with it so anyone reading can open the page and see the marks in place.
  - kind: new
    title: The annotation board
    text: >-
      A full-screen view of every annotation in the room, in columns by status. Drag a card to move it along, or walk
      it with the arrow keys. It is the same data the side panel shows, arranged for deciding what to pick up next
      rather than for reading one thread.
  - kind: new
    title: Approved
    text: >-
      A second ending to a thread, and it belongs to whoever asked for the change. Resolved says the work is done,
      approved says the requester agrees it is. Without it the person who fixed the thing was also the person who
      declared it finished.
  - kind: new
    title: Present to everyone
    text: >-
      Pull the whole room onto your scroll position instead of asking each person to click follow. It grants nothing a
      follower could not have set themselves, and any scroll or click releases them.
  - kind: new
    title: Hide the chrome
    text: >-
      Cmd+/ takes the toolbar and the panels off screen and leaves the annotations on the page. Figma's hide-UI, for a
      clean screenshot or an unobstructed look at what you are reviewing.
  - kind: new
    title: Your name and colour in one place
    text: >-
      Set both from a single card. Renaming yourself now carries the new name onto the annotations and replies you have
      already left, so a room does not end up half-signed by someone who no longer exists.
  - kind: improved
    title: Agents can see what was approved
    text: >-
      An agent reading a room can filter on approved, so it knows which fixes the requester has confirmed and stops
      asking about the ones that are already settled.
---

The gap this release closes is the one between leaving feedback and anyone
seeing it. An annotation was only ever as useful as the chance somebody opened
the link, so a room can now speak for itself in the channel where the work is
already being discussed, and a thread has an ending that the person who asked
for the change is the one to give it.

version: '0.10.1'
name: 'Drawing lands where the pen is'
date: '2026-10-30'
title: 'MarkLayer 0.10.1: Strokes Land on the Cursor on Zoomed Pages'
description: 'MarkLayer 0.10.1 fixes strokes drawn off the cursor on CSS-zoomed pages, marks that stayed put when scrolling mid-stroke, and text vanishing under the eraser.'
summary: >-
  Three fixes to drawing in the Chrome extension. On a page that zooms its own layout, strokes were drawn offset from the
  pen and ran off the screen; they now land under the cursor.
changes:
  - kind: fixed
    title: Strokes land under the pen on zoomed pages
    text: >-
      A page that sets CSS zoom on its root, such as 1.5, scaled the overlay with it. Every stroke was drawn at that
      multiple of where the pen was, so on a zoomed page it ran off the right and bottom edges and you could not see what
      you were writing. The overlay now cancels the page's zoom, and follows it if the page changes zoom later.
  - kind: fixed
    title: Marks move with the page while you draw
    text: >-
      Scrolling in the middle of a stroke left the marks already on the page where they were until you let go. They now
      move with the page under the pen.
  - kind: fixed
    title: Text stays while the eraser is active
    text: >-
      With the eraser selected, a redraw, for example from scrolling mid-stroke, could erase text annotations from the
      screen. Text is drawn normally again.

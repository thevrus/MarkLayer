changes:
  - kind: new
    title: Drawing tools
    text: Pen, highlighter, shapes, arrows and text, on any live page rather than on a screenshot of one.
  - kind: new
    title: Threaded comments
    text: Pin a comment anywhere on the page and reply to it in place.
  - kind: new
    title: Real-time collaboration
    text: Everyone in a room sees each annotation as it is made, over a WebSocket connection.
  - kind: new
    title: Shareable links
    text: The person opening your link needs no extension, no account and no email address.
  - kind: new
    title: PNG export
    text: Save the annotated page as an image for the places a link will not go.

MarkLayer started from one complaint: feedback on a website is given on a
screenshot of the website, and a screenshot cannot be clicked, scrolled, or
pointed at precisely. Everything since has been an attempt to keep the feedback
on the live page.

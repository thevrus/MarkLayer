version: '0.10.0'
name: 'Show it, then triage it'
date: '2026-10-12'
title: 'MarkLayer 0.10.0: Video Notes, Feedback Widget, Status Board'
description: 'MarkLayer 0.10.0 adds video notes on comments, a one-line script so visitors can comment on your live page, a status board, a zoomable device canvas and Trello.'
summary: >-
  Comments can now carry a short video of the camera or the screen, and a one-line script puts the same commenting on
  your own live site. Once the notes are in, a board shows what is open and who has it, and Trello, Notion, ClickUp and
  Zapier join the places a note can be filed.
changes:
  - kind: new
    title: Record a video note
    text: >-
      Some bugs are easier to show than to describe. A comment can carry up to two minutes of video from the camera or
      the screen. It plays in the thread and in the viewer, and an agent reading the room sees it labelled as a video
      note and treats it as something to act on. Text exports link to it.
  - kind: new
    title: Collect feedback on your own site with one script
    text: >-
      Paste a single script tag into a page, or a whole project, and visitors comment on the live site itself with no
      extension and no account. The share popover has a "Copy script" button and notes what a strict CSP needs to allow.
  - kind: new
    title: A board for the annotations on a link
    text: >-
      Open the board from the annotation panel to see every note by status. Filter it, and set a card's status or
      assignee from its menu.
  - kind: new
    title: File a note to Trello, Notion, ClickUp or Zapier
    text: >-
      Trello gets cards, Notion and ClickUp get a page or task, and Zapier takes the note as a webhook for anything
      else. That makes eight places a note can go.
  - kind: improved
    title: A device canvas you can pan and zoom
    text: >-
      The device overview is now one canvas: drag to pan, zoom between 5% and 400%, and use the fit-all and 100% keys to
      get back. The viewer's zoom readout and keys follow it.
  - kind: improved
    title: Priority is a menu
    text: >-
      Priority used to cycle through its levels on each click, so reaching "urgent" meant clicking past the rest. It
      opens a menu with the most urgent level first, and status and assignee read as labelled rows on every card.
  - kind: improved
    title: A clearer inspector
    text: >-
      The element inspector leads with the tag, id and size, puts the selector one click from your clipboard and folds
      the rest of the detail away until you want it.
  - kind: improved
    title: Settings show the real version
    text: >-
      The version in the extension's settings used to be a hardcoded "v0.3". It now reads the installed version, so a
      bug report can say which one you are on.
  - kind: fixed
    title: Loading a shared room checks what it gets back
    text: >-
      A shared room's annotations are now validated when they load, so a malformed response from the server is rejected
      instead of drawn onto the page.
---

Video notes and the widget share the composer, so a note recorded in the
extension, in the viewer or on a site carrying the script looks the same to the
person who reads it.

  - q: "Do I need to press Save?"
    a: "No. There is no Save button because there is nothing to save manually. On marklayer.app every mark and comment is written to the share link as you make it. In the extension, pressing Share is what publishes your marks to a link; before that they are only on your computer."
  - q: "Do the people I send the link to need an account?"
    a: "No. They open the link and can draw, comment and reply immediately, with no sign-up and no install. If you are both on the link at the same time you will see each other's cursors and changes live."
  - q: "Who can see my annotations?"
    a: "Anyone who has the link. The link is the access control, the same way an unlisted document link works: the code in it is random and unguessable, but it is not password protected. Keep links to sensitive work in private channels."
  - q: "How long do my annotations stay online?"
    a: "90 days from the last time anyone opened the link. Any view or comment resets that clock, so an active review round stays available for as long as it is active. After 90 days of silence the annotations are deleted for good. If you need a permanent record, download the comments as a Markdown file or export the page as a PNG from the viewer before the round goes quiet."
  - q: "Why does my share link show a blank or broken page?"
    a: "The share link reloads the original page inside MarkLayer, so it only works for pages that are publicly reachable on the internet. Pages behind a login, on localhost, or on an internal company network cannot be loaded that way. A handful of sites also refuse to load inside another page at all, including YouTube, Instagram, X, Facebook and TikTok. Your marks are still there in these cases, but the page underneath them may not render."
  - q: "Can I use MarkLayer on my phone?"
    a: "Not yet. Both the web app and the extension are desktop only, and marklayer.app will tell you so if you open it on a phone. Share links can be opened on a phone but are not designed for annotating there."

## Two ways to use it, and the difference that matters

**The website.** Go to [marklayer.app](https://marklayer.app), paste the address of the page you want to mark up, and press the arrow. MarkLayer loads that page inside itself and hands you the drawing tools. Nothing to install.

**The Chrome extension.** [Install it once](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/marklayer/fnfobegjifomgobgilaemihpcpidjamc), then click the MarkLayer icon on any page you are already looking at. This is the one to use for pages that need you to be logged in, like an admin panel or a client's dashboard, because you are annotating the real page in your own browser.

The difference that trips people up is *when the link gets created*.

On the website, the link exists first. The moment the page loads you are already at an address like `marklayer.app/s/V1StGXR8`, and every mark you make is saved to it as you go.

In the extension, your marks start out on your own computer only. They become a link when you press **Share**, and not before.

## What happens when you close the page

On the website: nothing is lost. Reopen the link and your marks are there, along with any comments other people left in the meantime.

In the extension: your unshared marks are saved on your own computer, tied to that exact page address. Open the same page again in the same browser and they come back with a small "Draft restored" note. That draft lives in this browser on this computer, so it will not survive clearing your browsing data, and it will not appear on your laptop if you drew on your desktop. Press Share to turn it into something that outlives the machine.

## How to get your link back

Every link looks like `marklayer.app/s/` plus a short random code.

1. Open your browser history and search for `marklayer.app/s`. Every page you shared will be in there.
2. Check where you sent it: the chat message, the email, the ticket.

If it is in neither place, it is gone. There is no account, so there is no list of your past links for us to look up, and support cannot recover one. The habit worth building: as soon as you create a link, paste it into wherever the work actually lives.

## Getting the feedback out

Comments do not have to stay in MarkLayer. In the panel on the right of any share link you can copy every comment and highlight as Markdown, or download it as a `.md` file, which paste cleanly into a ticket, a doc, or an AI coding assistant. You can also export the annotated page as a PNG image.

Do this before a review goes quiet if you need a permanent record, because the link itself is cleaned up 90 days after the last time anyone opened it.

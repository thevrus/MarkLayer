## Current published pricing

From [markup.io's pricing page](https://www.markup.io/pricing/), October 2026:

| Plan | Price | MarkUps/month | Users | Workspaces | Storage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pro | $79 / $99 / $129 a month | 50 / 75 / 100 | Unlimited | 1 | 100–200GB |
| Business | $499 / $649 / $799 a month | 200 / 400 / 600 | Unlimited | 1 | 500–600GB |
| Enterprise | Custom | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |

Annual billing takes two months off. Business adds branding, a vanity domain, a full metrics dashboard and priority support; Enterprise adds SAML SSO, SOC 2 documentation, and a dedicated success manager. Pro and Business come with a 14-day trial.

## What changed, and for whom it still works

Before 2025, Markup.io's free tier plus a $29 Pro plan made it the default recommendation for freelancers and small studios. The current structure inverts the value: $79 to $129 with unlimited users is genuinely competitive for a 20-person creative team living in one workspace, where per-seat rivals would bill several times that. The same price is hard to justify for a solo freelancer who ran on the free tier, and the one-workspace cap works against agencies that separate clients into workspaces.

That reshuffling, not the number alone, explains the migration pattern since: large single-workspace teams stayed, and the small and multi-client end went shopping.

## If you're deciding now

Run the seat math both ways. At 5 users, Markup.io's $79 entry tier beats Pastel's Team tier ($99+ plus per-seat) but loses to BugHerd's $50 Standard. At 15+ users in one workspace, the per-tier price usually wins among paid tools, as long as the team stays under 100 MarkUps a month; past that, Business starts at $499. And if what you actually need is annotation on live pages rather than a managed review pipeline, the free tier that Markup.io discontinued effectively lives on in [MarkLayer](/vs/markup-io): no accounts, no workspaces, unlimited everything, with the trade-off that there is no project management layer at all.
